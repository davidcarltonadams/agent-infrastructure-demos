microtonality

What is the harmonic series?

The set of integer multiples of a fundamental frequency (1f, 2f, 3f, 4f...). Its ratios are the raw material of just intonation.
Pyramid — for a non-technical listenerWhen one object vibrates — a plucked string, a column of air — it doesn't move at a single rate; it also resonates at whole-number multiples of its lowest frequency, all at once. That stack is the harmonic series, and it is why one note already contains many. In music those multiples line up with the intervals our ears hear as consonant.
science-trivia

What 2019 image was the first-ever direct picture of a black hole's shadow?

The Event Horizon Telescope image of M87's supermassive black hole, produced by a globe-spanning array of radio dishes acting as one Earth-sized telescope.
business-terms

What does 'total addressable market' (TAM) mean?

The full revenue opportunity if a product reached 100% of its market — the ceiling, before competition and real-world reach are accounted for.

Nonproliferation

Two Paths to Fissile Material

There are two routes to the material at the heart of a weapon. One enriches uranium in the isotope U-235, spinning uranium hexafluoride gas through cascades of centrifuges until the fraction of U-235 climbs from natural (under 1%) to weapons-grade (around 90%). The other breeds plutonium-239 by running natural or low-enriched uranium in a reactor, then chemically separating the plutonium from the spent fuel in a reprocessing plant. Each path leaves a different signature — centrifuge halls and hexafluoride handling on one, reactors and reprocessing on the other — and much of nonproliferation policy is the patient art of watching for both at once.
